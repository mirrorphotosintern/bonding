**Checked:** July 27, 2026  
**Status:** Working recommendation, not legal clearance

Keep **Bonding** as the repository/folder codename. Do not use it as the consumer brand.

No consumer name is currently recommended. **Jothe has been rejected.** It came from Kannada-specific company context that does not apply to this project, is not self-explanatory to a global audience, and should not appear in future product or brand work.

## Why not Bonding?

“Bonding” accurately describes the category, but that is also its weakness:

- it sounds like a feature or therapeutic category rather than a memorable product;
- it is difficult to own in search, app-store results, social handles, and conversation;
- it says what the outcome is, but not what makes this product distinctive;
- `bonding.com` is already registered;
- Cloudflare showed `bonding.app` as available only as a **$99.20 premium** registration, renewing at **$99.20**.

That premium price does not solve the underlying brand problem. It is not recommended.

## Coined and conversational-name exploration

The friendlier pattern behind names such as **HiGenie** and **Kida** is useful: short, easy to say, and more like a character than a category. The specific examples are not viable.

| Name | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Kida** | **Reject** | `hellokida.com` is already a live family-activity app with essentially the same moment-matching proposition. `kida.app` is also an established children's educational-app company. Exact and adjacent names have additional app and trademark collisions. |
| **HiGenie** | **Reject** | Cloudflare showed both `higenie.com` and `higenie.app` as registered. An active App Store app uses HiGenie, and the name appears across domestic-help, hygiene, medical-assistant, and other software/services. “Genie” also overpromises AI or infinite generation when this product's trust comes from reviewed activities. |
| **PlayKida** | **Do not pursue** | Cloudflare showed `playkida.com` available at $10.46, but adding a prefix does not cure the Kida product collision. |
| **WithKida** | **Do not pursue** | Cloudflare showed `withkida.com` available at $10.46, but it remains confusingly close to two child-focused Kida businesses. |

This exploration strengthens the case for an ownable, globally legible name—not a misspelling of “kid” and not a term that requires knowledge of a particular language. The next coined-name round should follow these constraints:

- two or three syllables;
- pronounceable after seeing it once;
- not begin or end with “kid”;
- not imply an AI chatbot or unlimited AI-generated activities;
- warm enough to address like a companion;
- clear of child/family apps before visual design begins;
- a credible `.com` available, even if it uses a natural prefix such as `with` or `hello`.

## Domain strategy

1. Do not purchase a domain until a globally legible consumer name survives product, app-store, web, and preliminary trademark checks.
2. Prefer a credible `.com`; a natural prefix is acceptable when it improves the spoken brand.
3. Do not buy several speculative domains before the name passes a proper clearance check.
4. Do not rename the `Bonding/` folder; changing the internal codename provides no user benefit.

Before public branding or purchase of expensive assets:

- search USPTO and relevant international trademark databases in the applicable software, education, entertainment, and subscription-service classes;
- check Apple App Store and Google Play name collisions directly;
- check the principal social handles;
- have counsel assess similarity, not just exact matches.

Domain availability and prices can change until checkout. Cloudflare availability is evidence that a domain could be registered at the time checked, not a reservation.

## Naming implication for the existing specs

The current documents use **Handful** as a working name. Until the user approves the change, treat:

- `Bonding` as the internal codename;
- `Handful` as the previous working name;
- no name as the approved consumer brand.

Once approved, update the product, content, technical, research, and design documents in one controlled pass so the old name does not leak into code identifiers or public copy.
